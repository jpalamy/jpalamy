## About

I am an education and technology specialist, educator, software developer, and digital innovation leader from Haiti. My work brings together pedagogical engineering, digital learning, software development, AI-enabled education, and education-sector strengthening.

I focus on turning educational and institutional challenges into practical, accessible digital solutions. My experience spans educational technology strategy, instructional design, teacher development, digital product development, humanitarian education, program quality, and Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL).

I have worked across education and development contexts for more than 15 years and have trained or supported more than 1,000 educators. I am particularly interested in technology that expands access to quality learning and strengthens the capacity of education systems in Haiti and beyond.

## Selected initiatives

### [Lekòl Konekte](https://lekolkonekte.com)
**Digital learning platform · Founder**

An online and distance-learning initiative designed to widen access to structured learning for students in Haiti and the Haitian diaspora. The platform's approach combines digital content, learning support, and technology adapted to local connectivity realities.

### [Mèt Patrik AI](https://metpatrik.com)
**AI-powered educational assistant · Founder**

An educational AI initiative designed to support learners in French and Haitian Creole through accessible, curriculum-oriented assistance. The project explores how conversational AI can contribute to learning support and educational inclusion.

### [SYGIPE](https://sygipe.com)
**Digital information and services platform**

A Django-based digital platform initiative bringing together educational information, resources, directories, and related services.

### DIDACTIA
**Curriculum design · Pedagogical innovation**

An educational development initiative focused on curriculum architecture, learning progression, age-appropriate content, and structured pedagogical resources.

### Responsible AI & Super Intelligence Training
**AI literacy · Capacity development**

A capacity-building initiative for YLAI and IVLP alumni and professionals in Haiti, focused on responsible use of emerging intelligent technologies and practical AI literacy.
