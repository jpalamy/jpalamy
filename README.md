## About

I am a Haitian education and technology specialist, educator, software developer, writer, and program leader. My work connects educational psychology, pedagogical engineering, digital learning, software development, artificial intelligence, institutional development, and humanitarian programming.

With more than 15 years of experience across education, technology, communication, and development, I focus on turning complex educational and institutional challenges into practical solutions. My experience includes teacher development, digital product development, curriculum and learning design, education-sector collaboration, program coordination, program quality, and Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL).

I have trained or supported more than 1,000 educators and contributed to education and technology initiatives in Haiti and internationally. I am committed to building accessible, locally relevant solutions that strengthen learning opportunities and education systems.

## Selected initiatives & platforms

### [Lekòl Konekte](https://lekolkonekte.com)
**Founder · Digital learning platform**

An online and distance-learning initiative designed to widen access to structured learning for students in Haiti and the Haitian diaspora. It brings together curriculum-oriented learning content, learner support, and technology adapted to local connectivity realities, including web, mobile, and offline-oriented access.

### [Mèt Patrik AI](https://metpatrik.com)
**Founder · AI-powered educational assistant**

An educational AI initiative designed to support learners in French and Haitian Creole through accessible, curriculum-oriented assistance. The project explores how conversational AI can contribute to learning support and educational inclusion.

### [SYGIPE](https://sygipe.com)
**Digital platform · Django-based development**

A digital platform initiative bringing together educational information, resources, directories, events, and related services.

### **EdTech Haïti**
**Education technology ecosystem**

An ecosystem-oriented initiative focused on connecting people, ideas, and solutions in educational technology in Haiti, and supporting the development and visibility of locally relevant EdTech initiatives.

### **Réponse pour l’Éducation (RPE)**
**Education · Humanitarian response · Institutional development**

A Haitian organization in the process of institutional structuring, committed to inclusive, equitable, and quality education. Its approach links emergency education, learning continuity, teacher support, child protection, community participation, MEAL, and educational innovation.

### **DIDACTIA**
**Curriculum design · Pedagogical innovation**

An educational development initiative focused on curriculum architecture, learning progression, age-appropriate content, and structured pedagogical resources.

### **Responsible AI & Super Intelligence Training**
**AI literacy · Capacity development**

A YLAI Impact Grant-supported capacity-building initiative for YLAI and IVLP alumni and professionals in Haiti, focused on responsible use of emerging intelligent technologies and practical AI literacy. The approved grant is **USD 1,815**, with implementation planned for November 2026–February 2027.

### **Omniscient Info**
**Editorial leadership · Media · Knowledge sharing**

A Haitian media platform where I serve as Editor-in-Chief. My editorial work includes the development and coordination of content that informs, analyzes, and contributes to public understanding, with a particular interest in education and social issues.
